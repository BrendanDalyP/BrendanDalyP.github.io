Hub & Spoke Integration Patterns

Implementation Approach

The hub & spoke model centralizes enterprise data in a governed warehouse (hub) while distributing domain-specific, optimized data marts (spokes) to business units. This enables:

  • Single source of truth - Centralized governance and data quality
  • Domain autonomy - Business units control their analytics layer
  • Performance optimization - Pre-aggregated, denormalized marts for fast queries
  • Scalability - Add new spokes without impacting existing workloads
